Waves

Waves move knobs for you: a slow sweep of the cutoff, a wobble on the resonance. Add one, pick or draw its shape, then send it to any knobs.

Song

Each column is one bar. Tap a letter to change that bar’s bass or drum pattern, so bass A can play over drums B. In Play song, the editors follow the bar that’s playing.

MIDI

Plug in a keyboard or controller, then tap Connect MIDI. Your browser will ask for permission; choose Allow. Hold a key to play the bass over the drums, play legato to slide, and hit hard to accent. Notes on channel 10 play the drums.
